# UnifiedLive

UnifiedLive is a media technology solutions company that integrates audiovisual systems, video conferencing, live streaming, broadcast, content distribution, storage, automation, and AI-powered audience engagement.

## Positioning

UnifiedLive is one partner for the entire live journey: strategy, production, distribution, and engagement brought together in one dependable delivery model.

The company bridges audiovisual integration, video conferencing, live streaming, and broadcast. It connects business communications, physical venues, remote participants, and online audiences.

## Services

| Service | Description |
|---|---|
| Media Technology Solutions | Ideation, solution architecture, project management, systems integration, workflow orchestration, WebRTC, and low-latency communication |
| Audiovisual Integration | AV system design and integration, meeting-room and venue AV, video conferencing integration, business communications, and venue-to-online connectivity |
| Live Event Production | Broadcast-quality production for on-premise, virtual, and hybrid events; multi-camera, remote, on-site, redundant, podcast, radio, IRL, cloud-based production, bonding encoding, and SRT relay workflows |
| Live Streaming & Distribution | Live-stream pipelines, multi-platform distribution, global CDN delivery, redundancy, cloud relays, and real-time streaming analytics |
