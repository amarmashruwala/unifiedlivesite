# About Unified Live

Unified Live is a media technology solutions company built around a simple idea: organizations should not have to manage audiovisual systems, video conferencing, live streaming, broadcast, distribution, storage, and audience engagement as disconnected silos.

We bring these capabilities together.

## What we do

We design, integrate, produce, distribute, automate, and support media technology solutions for the entire live journey.

Our work spans:

- Audiovisual integration
- Video conferencing and business communications
- Live event production
- Live streaming and multi-platform distribution
- Broadcast and remote production
- Cloud infrastructure and relays
- Content storage and workflow
- AI-powered automation and audience engagement

## How we work

Unified Live acts as a solutions architect and integration partner.

We start with the customer’s objective, then design the right combination of technologies, workflows, and delivery models. We integrate commercial platforms, cloud services, open-source applications, and custom automation where appropriate.

We do not lead with a fixed product. We build around the outcome the customer needs.

## Our approach

1. **Understand the objective** — Identify the audience, venues, participants, content, and desired outcome.
2. **Design the solution** — Create an architecture that connects AV, communications, production, distribution, and engagement.
3. **Integrate the technology** — Combine systems, workflows, cloud services, and open-source components.
4. **Automate where valuable** — Reduce repetitive manual tasks and improve reliability.
5. **Deliver and support** — Produce, distribute, monitor, and improve the live experience.

## Why Unified Live

- One partner for strategy, production, distribution, and engagement
- Equal expertise across AV integration, video conferencing, live streaming, and broadcast
- Experience integrating commercial, cloud, and open-source technologies
- Automation-first approach to repetitive media workflows
- AI-enabled engagement, moderation, insights, and voice interaction
- Solutions designed for on-premise, virtual, hybrid, and multi-venue environments
