# Unified Live Brand Guide

## Company name

- Primary name: **Unified Live**
- Do not use: “UnifiedLive” in public-facing website copy unless it is part of an existing domain, email address, or technical identifier.
- Capitalization: Unified Live

## Tagline

**One Partner for the Entire Live Journey**

## Positioning statement

Unified Live is a media technology solutions company that brings together audiovisual integration, video conferencing, live streaming, broadcast, content distribution, storage, automation, and AI-powered audience engagement.

We help organizations connect venues, remote participants, online audiences, and broadcast platforms through one dependable delivery model.

## Core message

**Bridging Audiovisual, Video Conferencing, Live Streaming & Broadcast**

Unified Live integrates the technologies that organizations use to communicate, produce, distribute, and engage. We connect boardrooms, venues, studios, remote teams, and global online audiences.

## Brand personality

- Expert and technically credible
- Innovative and forward-looking
- Clear and approachable
- Solution-oriented rather than product-led
- Reliable and partnership-focused

## Voice and tone

- Write in clear, confident, plain English.
- Lead with customer outcomes, then explain the capability.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon; explain technical concepts simply when needed.
- Use active voice.
- Prefer “we design, integrate, produce, distribute, and automate” over vague claims such as “we are the best.”

## Words and phrases to use

- Media technology solutions
- Audiovisual integration
- Video conferencing
- Live streaming
- Broadcast
- Hybrid events
- Multi-platform distribution
- Global CDN delivery
- Workflow automation
- AI-powered audience engagement
- Low-latency communication
- WebRTC
- Solutions architecture
- Systems integration
- One partner for the entire live journey

## Words and claims to avoid

- “Leading”
- “Best”
- “World-class”
- “Number one”
- “Guaranteed”
- “Unlimited”
- Unverified client names
- Unverified statistics
- Specific product endorsements
- Vendor logos or partner logos unless explicitly approved

## Visual direction summary

- Professional, modern, technology-forward
- Clean layout with generous white space
- Strong hierarchy and accessible contrast
- Visual themes: connectivity, control rooms, broadcast production, live events, cloud infrastructure, collaboration, and audience engagement
- Avoid generic stock photos of people pointing at screens
