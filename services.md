Unified Live provides media technology solutions across the full live journey: strategy, ideation, project management, production, distribution, storage, and audience engagement.

We are solutions architects and systems integrators. We combine commercial technologies, open-source applications, cloud services, and custom workflows to build reliable solutions for each customer.

- Technical consulting and delivery support

A clear, integrated technology roadmap that connects people, venues, content, and audiences.
