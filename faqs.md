# Unified Live FAQs

## What does Unified Live do?

Unified Live provides media technology solutions across the entire live journey: strategy, ideation, project management, content production, distribution, storage, and audience engagement.

## Do you only provide live streaming?

No. Live streaming is one important capability, but Unified Live also provides audiovisual integration, video conferencing, broadcast production, content storage, workflow automation, and AI-powered audience engagement.

## What is Unified Live’s core differentiator?

We bridge audiovisual integration, video conferencing, live streaming, and broadcast. We connect business communications, venues, remote participants, and online audiences through integrated media technology solutions.

## Do you work with hybrid events?

Yes. Unified Live supports on-premise, virtual, hybrid, and multi-venue experiences.

## Can you integrate existing AV and video conferencing systems?

Yes. We design and integrate AV, video conferencing, control, audio, video, network, and streaming systems to connect physical venues with remote participants and online audiences.

## Do you provide automation?

Yes. Automation is a major part of Unified Live. We provide standard live-stream pipelines and automate tasks that would otherwise require manual effort.

## What AI capabilities do you provide?

Capabilities can include AI workflows, workflow orchestration, live moderation, real-time analytics, live insights and highlights, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, live voice agents, polls, Q&A, and moderated chat.

## Do you support content creators?

Yes. Unified Live can support IRL streaming setups, cloud relays, SRT relays, bonding encoding, cloud OBS deployments, podcasting, radio broadcasting, and multi-platform distribution.

## Do you work with government organizations?

Yes. Unified Live serves business-to-government customers, including use cases such as public meetings, civic engagement, briefings, training, and hybrid consultations.

## Where does Unified Live operate?

Unified Live has a presence in Sydney, Australia, and Mumbai, India.

## Does Unified Live partner with other organizations?

Yes. Unified Live is open and eager to partner with businesses, organizations, technology providers, creators, and institutions to expand its network.

## Do you sell specific products?

The initial Unified Live website is service-led. Specific products may be introduced later where they support a customer’s required outcome.
