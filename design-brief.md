# Unified Live Website Design Brief

## Objective

Create a professional, responsive marketing website that clearly communicates Unified Live’s ability to integrate audiovisual technology, video conferencing, live streaming, broadcast, automation, and AI-powered audience engagement.

## Audience

- Business decision-makers
- Event and marketing teams
- IT and AV leaders
- Government and education stakeholders
- Healthcare organizations
- Content creators
- Potential technology and delivery partners

## Design principles

- Professional and credible
- Modern and technology-forward
- Clear, uncluttered, and easy to scan
- Mobile-first
- Accessible
- Fast-loading
- Service-led, not product-led

## Visual direction

Use a clean, contemporary design with:

- Strong typographic hierarchy
- Generous white space
- Clear section separation
- High-contrast accessible text
- Subtle motion only where it improves understanding
- Visual motifs of connectivity, signal flow, control rooms, broadcast production, cloud delivery, and live audiences

## Color and typography

The agent may propose a palette, but it must:

- Use accessible contrast ratios
- Avoid overly saturated or distracting colors
- Support a professional enterprise appearance
- Provide dark and light mode only if it does not complicate the build

Use a modern sans-serif typeface for headings and body text. Prioritize readability over decorative typography.

## Layout requirements

- Sticky or clearly accessible header
- Prominent Human View / AI Agent View toggle
- Clear primary call to action: Start a Project
- Service cards with concise descriptions
- Industry cards with relevant use cases
- About section explaining the convergence of AV, video conferencing, streaming, and broadcast
- Global presence section for Sydney and Mumbai
- Contact form with the fields defined in `contact.md`
- Footer link to AI Agent / Markdown Version

## Imagery direction

Use abstract, professional visuals related to:

- Audiovisual integration
- Video conferencing
- Broadcast control rooms
- Live event production
- Cloud and network connectivity
- Audience engagement
- AI and automation

Avoid:

- Generic corporate stock photography
- Misleading screenshots
- Fake client logos
- Fake testimonials
- Product-specific imagery

## Accessibility

- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard-navigable interface
- Visible focus states
- Descriptive alt text
- Sufficient color contrast
- Responsive layout from mobile through desktop
- Clear form labels and error states
